Python · Group Project · Dec 2025

Security Market Line & Volatility Forecasting

My contribution to a 4-person Computational Finance & Business Analytics group project analysing financial markets with quantitative methods. Built CAPM regressions across seven stocks (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, JPM, Barclays, J&J) against the S&P 500 to estimate beta and plot the Security Market Line, then modelled return forecasts (ARIMA) and volatility persistence (GARCH) for NVIDIA and Barclays.

Key finding: NVIDIA carried the highest beta (~1.8–2.0) and volatility persistence in the group, with an annualised return near 65% over the study window.

Excel · Dec 2025

GDP Growth: Statistical Analysis & Forecasting

A ten-part statistics exercise on US GDP growth — descriptive statistics, correlation and confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, simple and multiple regression with full diagnostics, and time-series forecasting using moving averages and MAE/MSE error metrics.

Key finding: diagnostics exposed the limits of a small predictor set for explaining GDP growth — a lesson in model specification and diagnostic testing rather than an inflated fit.
